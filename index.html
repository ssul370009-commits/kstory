K-Story Publisher

Private publishing tool · Operated by an individual creator in South Korea

K-Story Publisher is a small, private tool that the channel owner uses to publish short vertical videos to the owner's own YouTube and TikTok channels. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What the channels are about

Each day, the owner picks a story that is trending in Korean online communities, rewrites it in original wording, removes every name and identifying detail, and turns it into a 30–60 second video with stock footage, captions and an AI-generated voice. Each video is clearly labeled as AI-assisted.

What the tool does

  • Uploads a finished video file from the owner's computer to the owner's channel, with title, description and tags.
  • Sets a scheduled publish time for each country's evening hours.
  • Reads view counts of the owner's own videos to see which topics work.

The tool runs only on the owner's computer. It does not access any other person's account or data.
