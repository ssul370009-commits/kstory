Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 9, 2026

This policy explains how K-Story Publisher ("the App") handles information. The App is a private tool used only by its owner to publish videos to the owner's own YouTube and TikTok accounts.

1. Information the App accesses

  • YouTube (Google) account: through YouTube API Services, the App uploads videos to the owner's channels, sets video metadata and publish schedule, and reads basic statistics (such as view counts) of the owner's own videos.
  • TikTok account: through the TikTok Content Posting API and Login Kit, the App reads the owner's basic profile information (display name, avatar, open ID) and publishes videos to the owner's TikTok account.
  • OAuth access tokens issued by Google and TikTok after the owner signs in.

The App does not access, collect or store information about any other user, viewer or commenter.

2. How the information is used

Information is used only to publish the owner's videos and to show the owner how the owner's own videos perform. It is never sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train any model.

3. Storage and security

OAuth tokens and statistics are stored only on the owner's personal computer, in files that are not shared or uploaded anywhere. No server or database operated by the App holds this data.

4. Retention and deletion

Statistics are kept for as long as the owner needs them to plan content. Tokens and stored data can be deleted at any time by deleting the App's local files. Access can also be revoked at any time:

  • Google: Google security settings – third-party access
  • TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services permissions

5. YouTube API Services

The App uses YouTube API Services. By using the App, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

6. TikTok

Use of TikTok features is also subject to the TikTok Terms of Service and TikTok Privacy Policy.

7. Changes and contact
